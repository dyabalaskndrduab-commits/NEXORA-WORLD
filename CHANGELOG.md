# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — Expanded city districts
- Expanded the playable ground and movement boundary to a 140 × 140 city area.
- Added a wider avenue grid, outer residential towers, retail buildings, two pocket parks, and two destination landmarks.
- Added collision-aware movement around the main building footprints.
- Extended the camera far plane to keep the larger district visible.
- Reduced per-building window geometry to keep the expanded scene more suitable for mobile GPUs.
- Updated project documentation to distinguish the local 3D client from the separate development-only backend.
